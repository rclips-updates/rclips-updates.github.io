The details that save you time

Cutting

  • Lossless cutting

    Clips are cut without re-encoding, so the picture is exactly the original.

  • Frame-exact re-encode

    Need every clip to start on its shot? Re-encode to H.264, H.265 or ProRes.

  • Cut a single part

    Pick a start and an end on an Adobe-style timeline and save just that part.

  • Trim inside a shot

    Keep only the part of a shot you want before you export.

Built for editors

  • Filters for your edit

    Show only shots where your character is walking, turning, or still and quiet.

  • Sort your shots

    By time, best first, longest, or biggest face.

  • Change the file type

    MKV to MP4 or MOV without touching the picture, ready for After Effects.

  • One folder per character

    Export every character at once, each into their own folder.

  • “Other” group

    Shots without a known character go into their own group, so you can review them yourself.

  • One file per episode

    Join all the shots of an episode into one file, back to back with no gaps.

FAQ

Questions

Is the quality really the same as the original?

Yes. By default Rclips cuts without re-encoding, so every frame is the original picture. Re-encoding is only used if you choose it.

What files does it work with?

Episodes in MKV, MP4 or MOV, like Blu-ray Remux and WEB-DL files. Clips can be saved as MKV, MP4 or MOV.

Does it work with After Effects and Premiere?

Yes. Save as MP4 or MOV, or use Change file type to turn an MKV into an MP4 that After Effects opens.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs on your computer. Rclips only goes online to check for updates and to activate Pro. Monthly and yearly plans also check in once a day and keep working up to 14 days offline. Lifetime never needs to check in.

How does Pro work?

After you buy, you get a license key by email. Paste it into Rclips and Pro turns on for that computer.

Can I move Pro to a new computer?

Yes. Deactivate the old computer yourself from the Polar customer portal, then paste your key on the new one.

Windows says the app is not safe. Is it?

It is a normal warning for new apps from small developers. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Does Rclips work on Mac?

Not right now. Rclips is Windows only.
